Additional Project Features

1. Gamification & Milestone Badging

Target User: Student

Function Description:

Level and Title System: Implements a tiered segment system that automatically advances students to a higher rank or title when their accumulated points reach specific thresholds.

Badges: Unlocks exclusive badges for completing various types of activities. For example:

Academic Pioneer: Awarded for participating in 3 academic lectures in a row.

Community Star: Awarded for participating in volunteer service activities.

Progress Visualisation: Displays statistical charts in the personal center (utilizing charting libraries such as MPAndroidChart) to illustrate remaining points needed for the next stage goal, alongside radar charts showing multi-dimensional ability distributions (e.g., leadership, socialization, and innovation).

2. Smart Reminders and Check-in Alerts

Target User: Student

Function Description:

Reminders: Students receive push notifications 24 hours and 3 hours before a booked event.

Cancel from the Reminder: The 3-hour reminder includes a "Can’t make it" action button, allowing instant cancellation to automatically release the spot for the next student on the waitlist.

Check-in Alert: Students receive a notification when event check-in opens, with a direct tap-through to open the check-in screen.

Value: Reduces the rate of missed events and prevents wasted seat allocation.

Scope: Reminders operate offline (locally scheduled on the device). Email and SMS reminder channels are excluded from this release.
